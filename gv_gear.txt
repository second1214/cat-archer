Gear
From Archero 2 Wiki and Guides
Archero 2 includes various equipment that give stat bonuses to the hero when equipped and buff different aspects of their builds.

Gear[edit | edit source]
There are 6 types of equipment in the Archero 2, and you can equip one of each in its respective slot:
Weapon
Armor
Helm
Boots
Amulet
Ring
It is possible to level up equipment using Gear Scrolls & Gold that will add additional main stats to the gear. If another gear is equipped, the upgrades will carry over, so upgrades improve the gear slot.
Each piece of gear can have different quality's as well as additional effects that can boost specific builds.
Blacksmith[edit | edit source]
Blacksmith allows players to merge the gear in order to improve its quality. It will require either an exact copy (duplicate) of an item of the same quality tier or same type of item of the same quality tier. (Universal material can be used in place of any "same slot gear" when merging, see below table)
