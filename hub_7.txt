Gear Swap Guide
Everything about scroll swaps and series swaps — conditions, optimal timing, and key notes.
Published: 2026-05-03Updated: 2026-05-03
Scroll Swap
A scroll swap takes one spare S Epic gear piece you own and replaces it with a random S Epic piece from the same series. It does not swap your currently equipped gear.
You cannot predict which slot type you will get, so when and what you swap matters a great deal.

All other 5 slots

How It Works
•Random draw from the same-series S Epic pool — no guarantee of any specific slot type
•The gear you swap is immediately consumed (cannot be undone)
•The result keeps the same grade as the gear you used
•Getting a type you already have plenty of is entirely possible

When to Use Scrolls
✕
Before Chaotic — Not Recommended
•All 6 gear types drop randomly — certain types can pile up fast
•Even scrolling an excess piece gives no guarantee of the type you need
•A severe imbalance can significantly delay your Chaotic completion
✓
Just Before Chaotic — Recommended
•You can see exactly which gear types are missing at this point
•Scrolling only the excess pieces shortens the path to Chaotic
•Securing Godforge materials first lets you squeeze double value from each scroll
After the Godforge update, excess Chaotic gear can be dismantled as enhancement materials, which partially eases imbalances. However, Lv1 Rank 4 still requires dismantling ~24 S-grade pieces, so the problem is not fully resolved.

Series Swap
The series swap feature lets you convert your entire currently equipped set to a different series — for example, Oracle to Dragoon or vice versa.

Non-Chaotic pieces (e.g. Mythic+3) also transfer at their current grade. All 6 pieces at Mythic+3 or above meets the swap condition.
Requirements
•All 6 slots (Weapon, Necklace, Ring, Helmet, Armor, Boots) must be Mythic+3 or higher
•30-day cooldown must have elapsed since the last swap

Note
Weapon skins do not transfer on a series swap. If you own a skin, check this before swapping.
FAQ
QCan I use a scroll to swap an Oracle piece into a Dragoon piece?
No. Scroll swaps are within the same series only. An Oracle piece will always convert into another Oracle piece of a random slot type. To change series entirely, you need the Series Swap feature.QShould I use scrolls early or save them?
Save them until you are about to reach Chaotic grade. Using them carelessly before that can cause slot imbalances. Since the Godforge update, excess gear can be used as materials, which eases the problem — but the safest approach is still to use scrolls just before completing Chaotic.QDo enhancement levels carry over on a series swap?
Yes, Godforge enhancement levels transfer intact. Gear levels also carry over. However, weapon skins do not transfer, so be careful.QIs a series swap impossible before reaching Mythic+3 on all 6 slots?
Yes, all 6 slots must be Mythic+3 or higher for a series swap. If even one slot is Mythic+2 or below, the swap is unavailable. This condition generally requires at least 60 S-grade gear pieces.QWhy is the Arena Shop the best source for scrolls?
The Arena Shop offers one scroll per week at a 90% discount. Compared to other sources, the cost is far lower, so purchasing it every week without fail is strongly recommended.
