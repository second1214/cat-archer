Currency and Items
From Archero 2 Wiki and Guides
Archero 2 has various currencies that allow players to upgrade their hero, take part in the events and do gacha pulls for various rewards.

2 Gems

Gold[edit | edit source]
Gold is the main Currency in Archero 2. It is used to Draw Talent Cards and Upgrade Gear.
Gems[edit | edit source]
Gems are premium Currency in Archero 2. It is used to purchase in game items (Keys, Shards, Tickets, Shovels.
Gear Scrolls[edit | edit source]
Gear Scrolls are crafting resources in Archero 2. They are being used to upgrade Gear.
There are 6 types of Gear scrolls:
Weapon Scroll - used for Weapon Upgrades.
Armor Scroll - used for Armor Upgrades.
Helmet Scroll - used for Helmet Upgrades.
Ring Scroll - used for Ring Upgrades.
Amulet Scroll - used for Amulet Upgrades.
Boots Scroll - used for Boots Upgrades.
Chest Keys[edit | edit source]
Chest Keys are used to open Gear Chests in the Shop.
There are 3 types of Chest Keys:
Silver Chest Key - used to open Silver Chests.
Obsidian Chest Key - used to open Obsidian Chests.
Chromatic Chest Key - used to open Chromatic Chests/Mythstone Chests
Character Shards[edit | edit source]
Shards are resources in Archero 2 that are required to upgrade your characters
There are 8 types of character shards:
Alex Shard - used to upgrade Alex.
Nyanja Shard - used to upgrade Nyanja.
Helix Shard - used to upgrade Helix.
Seraph Shard - used to upgrade Seraph.
Dracoola Shard - used to upgrade Dracoola.
Rolla Shard - used to upgrade Rolla.
Otta Shard - used to upgrade Otta.
Atreus Shard - used to upgrade Atreus.
Other Items[edit | edit source]
these are other items that are used throughout the game, some of these are event dependent
Shovel - used in Rune Ruins.
Promised Relic Shovel - used in Promised Ruins.
Gold Cave Ticket - used in Gold Cave.
Seal Battle Ticket - used in Seal Battle.
Arena Ticket - used in Arena.
Sky Tower Ticket - used in Sky Tower.
Dice - used in the Monopoly event
Guild Gold - used in the guild shop to purchase items
Wish Token - used to draw wishes in the shop
Balloon Ticket - used in the Bingo event
Enchantium - used to enchant your Runes
