Magic Plant Defense Guide
Content for earning eggs that hatch into pets — boss mechanics, solo vs party play, egg drop rates per stage, and recommended builds.
Published: 2026-05-04Updated: 2026-05-04
What is Magic Plant Defense?
Magic Plant Defense is the content where you earn eggs that hatch into pets. Like Shackled Jungle, three bosses appear at fixed intervals, and you must protect the "plant" from each boss and the surrounding mobs.
Defeating the final (third) boss clears the run. Character Resonance, gear, runes, and Rune Enchantment all apply, so your full spec is directly reflected in performance.
What Applies
Unlike Shackled Jungle, every upgrade applies in Magic Plant Defense.

Gameplay Strategy
Clear out every mob rushing toward the plant. If the plant dies, the stage fails — and you get no eggs.
Bomb monsters charge in fast, so use freeze effects or strong burst damage to take them out quickly.
Drops on Monster Kill
Level-Up Star
A white star drops near the plant whenever you kill a monster. Picking it up speeds up the next boss spawn so you can clear faster.
Tip
If you don't have the firepower to take down a boss, skipping the stars and focusing on leveling up first is also a valid approach.
Warning
If you fail to the final (third) boss, you don't earn any eggs. You can, however, still get a partial Spirit Pact Seed reward.
Rewards — Egg Grade Rates
Rewards are rolled by stage-specific rates. You can randomly receive Spirit Pact Seeds and eggs (Rare / Epic / Legendary).
The higher the stage you clear, the higher your chance of pulling a higher-grade egg.
Spirit Pact Seed
Material used for pet Hall promotion. Earned together with eggs on clear.

Frequently Asked Questions
QI didn't get my weekly tickets.
If you already hold 3 or more tickets when Monday's grant runs, no additional tickets are given. If you hold 1 or 2, your total is topped up to 3 — not added on top. Use up your tickets before grant day each week to make sure you collect every grant.QWhich is more efficient — solo or party?
To collect the full weekly rewards you need to do both (10 each, 20 total). If your spec is strong enough, run solo for stable clears. If not, get a strong partner and ride along in party mode for an efficient bus.QAre there any rewards if I fail at the final boss?
You don't earn any eggs, but you can still get a partial Spirit Pact Seed drop. If you feel your boss damage is short, it's safer for total egg accumulation to stick with a stage you can reliably clear and farm the egg-grade rates there.QWhat is the white star that drops when I kill monsters?
It's a level-up / boss-spawn acceleration item. Picking it up speeds up the boss spawn timing. When you don't have the firepower to take down the boss, deliberately skipping the stars and focusing on clearing regular mobs to level up is also a solid play.
