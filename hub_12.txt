What the table does not show
The shard cost table is identical for all 101 mystlings. Quality and aspect do not change the 790 shards needed for 0→25★.
The 7 collab mystlings have no shard upgrade curve, so their stars cannot be raised with shards. They are upgraded only through event rewards.
Common and Uncommon mystlings gain ATK and HP directly per star. At 25★: Common ATK 192 / HP 768, Uncommon 368 / 1,472.
Epic and Legendary mystlings instead gain final damage dealt or final damage reduction. At 25★: Epic 2.76%, Legendary 9.2%.
For epic mystlings, the displayed star only rises to 21 across levels 0–24. Some levels raise stats without changing the star, so check the numbers.
