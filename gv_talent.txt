Talent Cards
From Archero 2 Wiki and Guides
Talent Cards are cards that you are able to collect in game that boost a wide variety of stats. Each card is able to be upgraded to a maximum of 5 (Apart from the Glory and Tactics cards as these are 1 maximum).
Below is a list of available talent cards, stats listed below are for 5 cards unless stated otherwise.
The second set of talent cards [2] is unlocked after collecting all cards from the first set.
