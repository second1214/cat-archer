Sky Tower Rewards Guide
Sky Tower is floor-based PvE content where reaching specific floors for the first time grants high-value cumulative rewards such as diamonds, enhancement scrolls, and materials. This page lays out the first-reach rewards by floor in a single table, serving as a guide to help you decide how high to aim.
Sky Tower is by far the single largest source of enhancement scrolls, so if scroll shortage is the bottleneck on your progression, the most efficient approach is to use this page's table and push as high as possible all the way to the top.Read more
Enemy stats climb steeply with each floor, so the floor you can clear is determined by your build strength. Rather than forcing through many floors at once, the most resource-efficient flow is to revisit Sky Tower each time you push your build with character breakthroughs and gear enhancement, and update your peak floor incrementally.
Frequently Asked Questions
QDo Sky Tower rewards reset over time?
The first-reach rewards shown on this page are one-time only. You cannot receive the reward for a given floor twice, so it is important not to miss any floor up to your peak. Daily and weekly bonus rewards are run as separate systems.QWhere do I farm enhancement scrolls if I am short?
Sky Tower is the single largest source. Use the table on this page to check which rewards drop at each floor and push to the highest floor you can reach first. Guild merchant and event shops can also be used as supplementary sources to speed up your accumulation.QHow high should I push for the best efficiency?
It depends on your build strength. Rather than forcing through one or two floors at a time, revisit Sky Tower each time you push your build up a tier and update your peak floor. The first target range is to keep going until your enhancement scroll supply is comfortable.
