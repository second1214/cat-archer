Godforge Guide
Godforge is a separate upgrade track for gear that has already reached Mythic+3 or higher. It opens up additional stat boosts and a Descent effect across five levels (Lv.1 to Lv.5), and it represents the endgame step for completing top-tier gear builds.
Three core materials drive the cost: Equipment Essence, Oracle Crystal, and Godforge Core. This page lists the per-level requirement for a single piece (Lv.0 → Lv.5) and the total to bring all six slots — weapon, necklace, ring, helmet, armor, boots — to Lv.5, so you can convert long-term planning into a concrete essence target right away.Read more
The disassembly table shows how much Equipment Essence each rarity returns when broken down. Common, Great, Rare, Epic, and S-grade gear all yield different amounts, so use this when deciding which rarities to feed into your essence pool.
Reaching Lv.5 activates that gear set's Descent effect. Descent is the core mechanic that drives the main weapon's combat cycle, with all six slot effects tied to the same set. Mixing different Descents halves several of the per-slot effects, so most builds commit to a single Descent set.
Godforge cannot start until a piece reaches Mythic+3, so reaching that grade through normal upgrade and fusion is a prerequisite. Once you arrive at Mythic+3, review what each level unlocks and plan your resource allocation accordingly.
Frequently Asked Questions
QWhich gear can be Godforged?
Only gear that has reached Mythic+3 or higher unlocks the Godforge menu. Below that grade the menu is locked, so reaching Mythic+3 through standard upgrade and fusion is the prerequisite.QWhat does each Godforge level do?
Each level adds a stat boost (such as +10% ATK or +10% Max HP), and reaching Lv.5 activates the gear set's Descent effect. Descent shapes the main weapon's combat cycle, which is why most players prioritize Lv.5.QHow much material does Lv.5 on all six slots cost?
The "All 6 slots total (×6)" table on this page lists the exact total Equipment Essence, Oracle Crystal, and Godforge Core needed to bring every slot to Lv.5. It accumulates the per-level costs precisely — not a simple ×6 — so you can use it directly as a long-term resource plan.QHow do I farm Equipment Essence?
Mainly by disassembling gear you no longer use. The "Disassembly Rewards" table on this page shows the per-rarity essence yield. Higher-rarity gear typically returns more essence, but think carefully before disassembling rather than feeding the piece into upgrade or fusion.QWhich Descent set should I push first?
It depends on your main character, runes, and overall build synergy. Most players prioritize the sets popular in current PvP and PvE meta (Oracle, Griffin, etc.). Compare the Descent effects on this page and start by raising the set that most strongly amplifies your build's damage cycle to Lv.5.
