Artifact shard sourcesBased on v9, 2026-09-17
Vibrant Voyage (bingo) badge shop: 1 of 14 artifact shards for 2,400 badges (180 buys), a legendary artifact for 1,800 badges (24 buys), 2 universal epic artifact shards for 120 badges.
Starfall Disc Ingot shop: 1 of 12 artifact shards for 3,000 Ingots (180 buys), 1 of 4 legendary artifacts for 2,250 Ingots (24 buys), universal epic artifact shards for 75 Ingots.
Both events give 10 universal legendary artifact shards at the 200-draw milestone and a choice of 1 of 14 artifact shards at 400 and 500.
Regular bingo tiles also drop rare and epic artifact shards. Most are rare and epics are uncommon, so treat this as a secondary source.
Artifact Shard Usage Guide
Artifacts are a separate permanent stat axis from characters — a long-term investment whose effects apply to your stats regardless of which character you bring. Artifact shards are spent on artifact star breakthroughs, and higher tiers grant proportionally larger bonus effects. This page summarizes the per-step shard requirements and cumulative totals for legendary and mythic artifacts.
Artifact breakthroughs require fewer shards per single step than character breakthroughs, but legendary artifacts accumulate cost quickly, so it is important to plan which artifact to breakthrough first. Use the quick lookup at the top of the table to pick your current and target tiers and instantly see the shards required for that range.Read more
Unlike characters, artifacts share their effect across all characters simultaneously — they are a long-term investment that carries over even when you switch your main. For early- and mid-game progression, where main characters change frequently, allocating resources to artifact breakthroughs results in less wasted investment overall.
Legendary and mythic artifacts differ in both per-step shard cost and effect magnitude. Mythic artifacts are more valuable but their cumulative cost is also high, so it is generally most efficient to breakthrough 1-2 core mythic artifacts first while filling the rest with legendaries.
Frequently Asked Questions
QAre artifact shards the same resource as character shards?
No. Artifact shards are a separate resource from character shards, with different sources. Because character breakthroughs and artifact breakthroughs do not compete for the same resources, you can progress both at the same time.QWhich artifact should I level first?
Prioritize activating and star-breakthrough'ing artifacts so that their set bonus is enabled. Some artifacts are strong even without a set bonus, but this varies by meta — in most cases targeting the set bonus first is the more stable choice.QIs one mythic artifact or several legendary artifacts more efficient?
For the average player, mythic artifacts are much harder to obtain than legendaries. Set mythic artifacts as your top priority, but use your legendary shards to also strengthen the necessary legendary artifacts to fill the gaps in your build.QDo artifacts have key thresholds like characters do at ★1/★4/★7?
Artifacts strengthen their set bonus at every multiple of 3 (0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15), so the key thresholds differ from characters. For mythic artifacts, costs increase sharply at the 8 → 9 and 14 → 15 ranges — keep that in mind when planning.
