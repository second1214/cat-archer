Characters
From Archero 2 Wiki and Guides
In Archero 2, characters are your playable heroes, each offering unique abilities that shape your gameplay experience. You can find and manage them within the Gear menu on the Characters Tab. Unlocking new characters requires obtaining 50 of their specific shards, which are acquired through in-game quests and activities, or by purchasing them from the shop and during special events.
Currently, 8 characters are available in Archero 2:
Alex
Nyanja
Helix
Hela
Mymu
Hou Yi
Seraph
Dracoola
Rolla
Loki
Phynx
Nezha
Otta
Atreus
Thor
Cleo
Wukong
A key feature for character progression is Resonance, available at 3 and 6 levels. This allows you to select an additional skill from another unlocked character, providing significant build customization.

Alex's basic skin
Alex[edit | edit source]
Alex is a brave demon hunter, recognizable by his family's signature white hair. When monsters threaten, he fearlessly wields his Demon Hunter's bow and sets off on adventure without hesitation.
Powerful HP Recovery: Alex possesses strong self-healing capabilities.
Hunter Bloodline Awakening: His unique bloodline awakens over time in battle, granting him enhanced combat prowess.
Descendant of Atreus: Alex is a direct descendant of the renowned bow hero, Atreus.
