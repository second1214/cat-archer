Sky Tower
From Archero 2 Wiki and Guides
Sky Tower is one of the Events in Archero 2. You will have to fight progressively harder opponents on each floor in order to get the rewards.
Tower floors victories reward players with Gems, Ruin Shovels and Gear Scrolls.
Every 25 Floors you will also get additional Gems, Obsidian Chest Keys, and extra Ruin Shovels.
Each attempt cost 1 Sky Tower Ticket. You will get 3 Tickets per day and will be able to get up to 3 per day by watching ADs.
Sky Tower also has a Leaderboard, but there are no rewards, just a vanity metrics to see who is the strongest on the Server.
Sky Tower Combat[edit | edit source]
Every time you enter Sky Tower you will need to beat 5 floors.
After you defeat a floor you will encounter Valkyrie. 
As you enter the new floor you will be able to choose a new ability.
If you get defeated midway you will have to start again, completed floors won't count until all 5 Floors are finished.
Sky Tower Tips[edit | edit source]
Here are some Sky Tower Tips to help you finish the event easier and get more rewards:
Sky Tower fights include multiple enemies per floor. There will be a lot of Enemies, and it can be tricky to deal with all of them.
