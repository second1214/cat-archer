About Archero 2 Wiki[edit | edit source]
Archero 2 Game Vault Wiki is a community-driven website that aims for collecting as much information on Archero 2 as possible. Anything from the Beginner Guides with tips and tricks for the new players that will help to get a good game start to the advanced mechanics tips with thorough end-game guides. Guides contain links to ingame wiki items, that can help you to get as much information about the game as you need. It's up to you how to use this, as reference or a full-on guide.
Our goal is to be the most detailed Archero 2 wiki there is, with deep articles on each topic: every item, equipment piece, skill, build and game mechanic.
Welcome to the new era of the legendary roguelike mobile game — Archero 2! The once-great hero has fallen into the Demon King's trap and turned into an even more powerful leader of dark forces! As a new-generation hero, you must master every skill you acquire to embark on your mission to save the world!
