Frequently Asked Questions
QWhich gear slot should I enhance first?
Gear is split into two lines: weapons (weapon, necklace, ring) boost ATK, while armor (helmet, chest, boots) boost HP. Neither line is strictly more efficient — if you're lacking damage, focus the weapon line; if survivability is the issue, spread enhancements more evenly.QWhat's the difference between fusion and enhancement?
They are entirely separate systems. Fusion consumes gear materials to craft a higher-grade piece, while enhancement consumes gold and enhancement scrolls to raise the stats of gear you already have. You run both in parallel.QHow do I start Godforge?
You need at least a Mythic+3 piece to begin Godforge. Check the Godforge page for per-set material requirements and step costs so you can prepare before you start.QHow do I get S-tier gear?
S-tier gear is obtained mainly by drawing equipment with Chromatic Chest Keys, or through event shops and package purchases.QWhen should I disassemble gear?
Once you have built up Chaotic-grade gear, the spare S-grade pieces you no longer use can be disassembled into Equipment Essence for Godforge.
